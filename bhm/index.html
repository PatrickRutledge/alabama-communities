How to read this

  • Medicare does not record where a beneficiary lives. Claims identify the service billed, never the residence, and no public CMS file splits spend by residential setting. The supply figures here come from Alabama licensure; the Medicare figures are county-wide. They are joined by county, never by person.
  • Independent living is not licensed in Alabama and has no registry, so it appears nowhere in the bed counts. The 75+ and 85+ populations set against licensed beds are the closest available proxy for that market.
  • “Bed gap” is a relative measure, not a demand forecast. It is the arithmetic to reach the metro's own current rate — a benchmark drawn from this data, not a projection of need. A county at the metro rate is not necessarily well served; it is average.
  • Memory care beds are a separate Alabama licence (Specialty Care Assisted Living Facility) and are counted in addition to assisted living beds, not inside them. A campus holding both licences appears twice, correctly, with two bed counts.
