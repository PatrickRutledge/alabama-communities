Alabama Senior Living Bed Map

Every licensed assisted living and memory care community in Alabama, placed on the map and sized by licensed beds. Counties are shaded by total capacity. Zoom into a hotspot to read community names, bed counts, licence class and administrator.

The map

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, click a county to fly to it, or press + and − with the map focused. Dots hold their size on screen as you zoom, so clustered communities pull apart instead of staying stacked. Names appear past 2.5×. Click any dot for the full record.

Communities

On the dots. 262 of the 292 communities geocode to a street address. The remaining 30 sit on newer streets the Census address file does not yet carry; those are placed at the centre of their own city (24) or county (6) and are drawn with a hollow ring rather than a solid fill. Bed counts, names, licence class and administrators are exact in every case — only the pin position is approximate. On the licence classes. Alabama licenses memory care separately as a Specialty Care Assisted Living Facility, and many campuses hold both an ALF and a SCALF licence at one address — two licences with two separate bed counts. Rather than stack one dot invisibly on top of the other, co-located licences are nudged onto a tiny ring, so zooming in pulls them apart and both stay clickable. The record for each carries a badge saying how many licences share the address.
